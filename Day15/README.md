## 🎯 Problem 1: Find Maximum and Minimum

### Objective
Find the maximum and minimum elements in an array.

### Example

Input:
5
10 25 7 40 15

Output:
Maximum = 40
Minimum = 7

### Time Complexity
O(n)

### Space Complexity
O(1)

---

## 🎯 Problem 2: Reverse an Array

### Objective
Reverse an array using the two-pointer approach.

### Example

Input:
5
10 20 30 40 50

Output:
50 40 30 20 10

### Time Complexity
O(n)

### Space Complexity
O(1)

---

## 💡 Key Learning

The two-pointer approach uses two positions:
one at the beginning and one at the end.

The elements are swapped and the pointers move
towards the center.
